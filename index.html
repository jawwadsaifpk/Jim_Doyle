About Me

Passionate About Building Strong Foundations

I'm Jim Doyle, a dedicated Mathematics tutor based in West Hartford, Connecticut, United States with over 8 years of hands-on teaching experience. I hold a Master's degree in Mathematics from the University of Agriculture Faisalabad and have helped students from Grade 6 through Intermediate (FSc/ICS) achieve their academic goals.

My teaching approach focuses on building genuine understanding rather than rote memorization. I break down complex concepts into simple, relatable steps — making even the toughest topics feel manageable and interesting for every student.

I offer both home tutoring across Hartford and live online sessions via Zoom, making quality education accessible wherever you are. Whether your child needs to catch up, keep up, or get ahead — I'm here to help.

"My goal isn't just to help students pass — it's to make them fall in love with learning."
